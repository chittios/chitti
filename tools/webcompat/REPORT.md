- **test262 harness** runs `js_bc` (bytecode). Remaining SKIPs need `instanceof`/`Date`/`eval`/`class`/prototypes/async/etc.

- **Full engine `browser::js` + DOM**: createElement/appendChild/removeChild/querySelector(All)/getElementsBy*/classList/dataset/attrs/events/innerHTML/Node tree links; plus arrow/class/BigInt/RegExp/objects/arrays.
- **Native Cranelift JIT** from just is **not** in-kernel (no_std, dual-arch, no RWX).
